Milestones that move us forward.

Origin / 1968
1968

Intel is born

Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore found NM Electronics, soon renamed Intel.

The spark: A new company dedicated to making semiconductor memory practical.

First / 4004
1971

The first microprocessor

Intel introduces the 4004, the world's first commercially available microprocessor.

Why it mattered: Computing power moved from a room-sized machine onto a tiny chip.

Architecture / 8086
1978

The x86 foundation

The 8086 launches the architecture that would become a backbone of personal computing.

The legacy: x86 compatibility still powers an enormous part of the digital world.

Scale / 386
1985

32-bit computing arrives

The Intel 386 brings powerful 32-bit processing and multitasking to a wider audience.

The shift: A more capable processor opens the door to richer software and interfaces.

Inside / Pentium
1993

Pentium goes mainstream

Pentium turns performance into a household idea and makes the Intel Inside mark iconic.

The impact: Personal computers become a visible part of everyday life.

Efficiency / Core
2006

Core changes the balance

The Intel Core family raises the bar for performance per watt across laptops and desktops.

The design: Efficiency becomes as important as raw speed.

Structure / 3D
2011

Transistors go 3D

Intel begins high-volume production of 3D Tri-Gate transistors.

The breakthrough: A new transistor shape improves performance and energy efficiency.

Compute / AI
2023

AI becomes a platform

Intel expands its silicon, software, and systems portfolio for the era of AI everywhere.

The ambition: Make intelligent computing more open, accessible, and efficient.

Renewal / 2030
2030

Operations turn net positive

Intel targets renewable electricity, zero total waste to landfill, and net positive water goals.

The promise: Technology progress should also create measurable environmental progress.

Together / 2040
2040

Net zero, together

Intel aims to reach net zero greenhouse gas emissions across its value chain.

The horizon: Technology that helps people and the planet thrive together.

Beyond the chip

Progress that leaves a lighter footprint.

Intel's environmental work is designed to reach beyond its own facilities, improving the systems, communities, and supply chains that make modern computing possible.

01 / Climate

Decarbonizing how chips are made

Intel has invested in renewable electricity, energy efficiency, and lower-carbon operations across its global manufacturing footprint. The 2030 ambition is to power global operations with 100% renewable electricity and continue reducing emissions throughout the value chain.
